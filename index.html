About Me

I am a Research Scientist at NVIDIA Cosmos Lab, where I work on world foundation models and physical AI. I received my Ph.D. in Computer Science from Brown University, advised by Prof. Chen Sun, and my bachelor's degree in software engineering from Tsinghua University.

My research interests involve building physically grounded, reasoning-capable vision-language and world models, and exploring how they can be integrated into the physical world. Previously, I worked at Salesforce AI Research, Meta GenAI, and Google DeepMind as a research intern. Feel free to contact me for collaborations and casual chats.
